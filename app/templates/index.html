Questions

What can you see in my email?
Only the From, To and Subject lines. {{ app_name }} never opens message bodies or attachments, never sells data, and never uses your mail to train AI models.
Why does Google warn me when I connect?
Apps that can move Gmail messages go through a Google security review. Until ours is complete, Google shows a warning during sign-in.
What if a real email gets caught?
Open Activity, click “Not spam”, and the message goes back to your inbox. That sender is added to your safe list automatically.
How do I stop?
Disconnect Gmail or delete your account in Settings. Either one revokes our access at Google immediately.
